Crypto Exchange

A crypto exchange is a marketplace where people can buy, sell, and trade crypto assets, including cryptocurrencies, fiat currencies, and NFTs.

Types of Crypto Exchanges

Centralized Exchange (CEX)

Centralized exchanges act as an intermediary between a buyer and a seller. They generate revenue through commissions and transaction fees.

Decentralized Exchange (DEX)

A decentralized exchange allows buyers and sellers to transact directly from their digital wallets without going through an intermediary.

Hybrid Exchange (HEX)

A hybrid exchange combines the capabilities of CEX and DEX while aiming to provide the liquidity of a CEX with the privacy and security of a DEX.
