Family Safety

Official Government of India resources for reporting cybercrime, protecting mobile connections, and accessing cyber-safety services.

Cybercrime portal

The National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal allows citizens to report cybercrime online. Use the portal to report incidents involving financial fraud, online abuse, identity theft, hacking, phishing, and other cyber offences.

  • Report cybercrime online
  • For immediate danger or emergencies, contact local law enforcement or emergency services.
  • Keep transaction records, phone numbers, email addresses, messages, screenshots, and other evidence before submitting a report.

Sanchar Saathi

Sanchar Saathi is an official Department of Telecommunications initiative that provides mobile-safety services. It helps users check mobile connections issued in their name, report suspected fraudulent or unwanted communications, and block or trace lost mobile devices.

  • Open the Sanchar Saathi portal
  • Use the official portal to access the Sanchar Saathi services and mobile application information.
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, banking credentials, or identity documents with anyone claiming to provide support through an unofficial channel.

Official cybersecurity resources

  • RBI Cyber Security Framework in Banks: Reserve Bank of India guidance for strengthening cyber security controls, governance, monitoring, incident response, and customer protection in banks.
  • SEBI Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework: Securities and Exchange Board of India guidance for cybersecurity and cyber resilience among SEBI-regulated entities.
  • CERT-In: Indian Computer Emergency Response Team advisories, vulnerability notes, alerts, incident-response information, and cybersecurity awareness resources.
  • Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C): Ministry of Home Affairs resources for coordinated cybercrime prevention, reporting, awareness, and law-enforcement support.

General guidance

  • Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money or payment details were involved.
  • Change compromised passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Report suspicious messages, calls, websites, and social-media accounts through the relevant official service.
