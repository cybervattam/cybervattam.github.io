Credit Card Fraud

Credit card fraud occurs when card details are used without the cardholder’s permission. Details may be stolen through phishing, unsafe websites, malware, skimming, data breaches, or social engineering.

Warning signs

  • Transactions you do not recognise.
  • Unexpected verification messages or replacement-card notices.
  • Requests for the card number, expiry date, security code, PIN, or one-time code.

Stay safe

  • Keep card details and one-time codes confidential.
  • Use secure websites and avoid entering details after following an unsolicited link.
  • Enable transaction alerts, review statements, and set sensible card limits.
  • Contact your card provider immediately to block or cancel a card when fraud is suspected.
