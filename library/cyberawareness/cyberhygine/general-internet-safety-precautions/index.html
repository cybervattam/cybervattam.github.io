General Internet Safety Precautions

Unsafe internet use can expose people and organizations to phishing, viruses, trojans, worms, ransomware, business email compromise, and financial loss.

Do

  • Be cautious when clicking links or downloading from suspicious URLs.
  • Clear browser history after confidential activities or transactions.
  • Configure appropriate security and privacy settings for cloud storage.
  • Verify the authenticity and identity of social media profiles before corresponding with them.
  • Use services that request location information judiciously.
  • Avoid posting photos with GPS coordinates.
  • Check advertisements and sponsored content on search results and websites.
  • Verify the source and authenticity of content before sharing it.

Do not

  • Use public computers or public Wi-Fi for online shopping, internet banking, UPI, or other financial transactions.
  • Enter email addresses, phone numbers, or payment-card details on untrusted and unsecured websites.
  • Trust or share unverified content from social media, messaging apps, email, text messages, or phone calls.
  • Respond to messages asking for passwords or other sensitive information without verifying the source.
