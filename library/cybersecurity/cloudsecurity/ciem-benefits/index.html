CIEM Benefits

CIEM Benefits

Cloud Infrastructure Entitlement Management delivers multiple operational and security advantages.

  • Granular cross-cloud visibility of entitlements from a single dashboard
  • Stronger overall security posture
  • Enforcement of least privilege
  • Reduction of unused permission risk
  • Monitoring, detection, and remediation of anomalies
  • Support for DevOps speed and agility without sacrificing security

Outcome

CIEM allows organizations to manage identity risk in a scalable and consistent way across multi-cloud environments.
