About Us

Welcome to Cybervattam—an open community initiative dedicated to creating and sharing technology and cybersecurity knowledge in Indian languages, empowering everyone to learn core technical concepts in their native language.

Our library is currently available in English, Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu, Kannada, and Hindi, and we are actively working to expand into additional Indian languages.

Translation & Localization Approach

Articles are primarily authored in English and localized across supported languages. Cybervattam leverages AI translation tools combined with community review. Because automated translation can occasionally miss context or nuance, we continuously refine our content through human proofreading. Spot an awkward term or phrasing? Suggest an edit on GitHub or Review our Indic Tech Glossary.

How You Can Contribute

Whether you are a technologist, educator, translator, or native speaker, you can help make technical knowledge accessible in every Indian language:

  • Fix a translation: Submit a GitHub Pull Request, open a quick edit issue, or send us feedback via our Contact Us page.
  • Write an article: Author a new guide or lesson in English or directly in your native script via the Cybervattam GitHub repository.
  • Suggest a regional technical term: Propose natural, accurate native-language equivalents for cybersecurity and emerging technology terms by submitting a glossary suggestion.
