### Zero Trust Value Proposition

Zero trust brings measurable security and business value, especially in hybrid and remote work environments.

### Benefits

- Secure and seamless modern workplace for hybrid and remote workforces
- Improved business agility for teams building products and services across multi-cloud and hybrid environments
- Improved security posture due to reduced lateral movement
- Smaller attack surface
- Centralized visibility and policy enforcement

### Outcome

Zero trust helps organizations modernize access control while reducing their exposure to identity-driven and network-based attacks.
