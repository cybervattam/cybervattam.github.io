### Consumer Identity and Access Management (CIAM)

Consumer Identity and Access Management (CIAM) is a specialized type of identity management system.

It helps organizations securely register and manage consumer or customer identities and profile data.

It also helps control customer access to applications and services in business-to-consumer environments.

Regardless of which channel a customer uses, such as a website, mobile application, or telephone, CIAM helps provide a secure and seamless customer experience.
