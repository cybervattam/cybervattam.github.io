### Fungible Assets

Fungible assets are interchangeable and identical in value. One unit is essentially the same as another.

Examples of fungible assets include:
- Cryptocurrency
- Stablecoins
- Government-issued currencies
- Commodities represented in tokenized form

### Key Principle

Fungibility means that each unit can be replaced by another identical unit without affecting value or utility.
