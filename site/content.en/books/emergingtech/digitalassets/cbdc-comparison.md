| Attribute / Asset | Central Bank Note (Cash) | Central Bank Reserves | Deposits | CBDC | Stablecoins | Bitcoin | Ether |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Central Bank Liability | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No | No | No |

### Insight

CBDCs, stablecoins, and cryptocurrencies differ in legal status, backing, governance, and accessibility, which determines their practical use in financial systems.
