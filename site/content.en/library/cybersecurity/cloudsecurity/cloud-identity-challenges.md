### Cloud Identity Challenges

Cloud environments continue to grow, which makes it difficult for security teams to monitor and manage identities and their access privileges effectively.

### Major Challenges

#### Lack of Visibility
Security teams often lose visibility of all identities active across the environment.

#### Inconsistent Security Mechanisms
Different cloud services often use different security policies and IAM capabilities, creating governance inconsistency.

#### Permissions Gap
Organizations frequently assign excessive permissions rather than following least privilege, creating unnecessary exposure.

#### Inactive Identities
Some identities retain access to resources they no longer use, increasing the risk footprint.

### Impact

These challenges increase the risk of unauthorized access, data exfiltration, privilege escalation, and poor compliance posture.
