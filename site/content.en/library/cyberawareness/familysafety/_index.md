Official Government of India resources for reporting cybercrime, protecting mobile connections, and accessing cyber-safety services.

## Cybercrime portal

The [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/) allows citizens to report cybercrime online. Use the portal to report incidents involving financial fraud, online abuse, identity theft, hacking, phishing, and other cyber offences.

- [Report cybercrime online](https://www.cybercrime.gov.in/)
- For immediate danger or emergencies, contact local law enforcement or emergency services.
- Keep transaction records, phone numbers, email addresses, messages, screenshots, and other evidence before submitting a report.

## Sanchar Saathi

[Sanchar Saathi](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/) is an official Department of Telecommunications initiative that provides mobile-safety services. It helps users check mobile connections issued in their name, report suspected fraudulent or unwanted communications, and block or trace lost mobile devices.

- [Open the Sanchar Saathi portal](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/)
- Use the official portal to access the Sanchar Saathi services and mobile application information.
- Do not share OTPs, passwords, banking credentials, or identity documents with anyone claiming to provide support through an unofficial channel.

## Official cybersecurity resources

- [RBI Cyber Security Framework in Banks](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=11446): Reserve Bank of India guidance for strengthening cyber security controls, governance, monitoring, incident response, and customer protection in banks.
- [SEBI Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/jun-2024/cybersecurity-and-cyber-resilience-framework-for-sebi-regulated-entities_84475.html): Securities and Exchange Board of India guidance for cybersecurity and cyber resilience among SEBI-regulated entities.
- [CERT-In](https://www.cert-in.org.in/): Indian Computer Emergency Response Team advisories, vulnerability notes, alerts, incident-response information, and cybersecurity awareness resources.
- [Indian Cybercrime Coordination Centre (I4C)](https://i4c.mha.gov.in/): Ministry of Home Affairs resources for coordinated cybercrime prevention, reporting, awareness, and law-enforcement support.

## General guidance

- Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money or payment details were involved.
- Change compromised passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
- Report suspicious messages, calls, websites, and social-media accounts through the relevant official service.
