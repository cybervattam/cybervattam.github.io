Quishing is phishing delivered through a QR code. A fraudulent code may open a fake login page, trigger an unsafe download, or send you to a payment site controlled by a criminal.

## Warning signs

- The code arrives unexpectedly or is paired with urgency or threats.
- It is pasted over a legitimate code in a public place.
- The destination address looks unusual or asks for sensitive details.

## Stay safe

- Preview and inspect the web address after scanning before continuing.
- Type addresses manually for sensitive logins and payments.
- Download apps from official app stores rather than QR links.
- Keep devices updated, use multi-factor authentication, and avoid scanning unknown codes.
