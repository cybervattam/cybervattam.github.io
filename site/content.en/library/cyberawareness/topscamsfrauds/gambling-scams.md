Gambling scams use fake online betting, casino, or slot-style websites and apps. They may promise free spins, large bonuses, or guaranteed winnings but are designed to collect money and personal details.

## Warning signs

- The operator cannot be verified on the Australian Communications and Media Authority register.
- Payment is requested by direct transfer, PayID, cryptocurrency, or card only.
- Withdrawals are delayed, blocked, or require extra fees.
- The offer is promoted through social media with unrealistic winning claims.

## Stay safe

- Verify that the operator is licensed before registering or paying.
- Do not share passwords, PINs, or one-time codes.
- Stop using a suspicious app, change related passwords, and report the activity to your bank and relevant authorities.
