A remote access scam tricks you into installing software or granting control of your computer or phone. The criminal may steal information, install malware, or access online banking.

## Warning signs

- An unexpected caller or pop-up claims your device has a virus.
- Pressure to act immediately or pay with gift cards or unusual methods.
- A request to install remote-control software or log in to your bank.
- The cursor, settings, files, or performance change unexpectedly.

## Stay safe

- Never grant access to an unknown person who contacts you unexpectedly.
- Initiate support yourself through a verified official channel.
- Keep operating systems, security software, and applications updated.
- If access was granted, disconnect from the internet, scan the device, change sensitive passwords from another device, and contact your bank.
