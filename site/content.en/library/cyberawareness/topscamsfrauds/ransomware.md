Ransomware is malicious software that encrypts files or blocks access to a device and demands payment. Criminals may also steal data and threaten to publish it.

## How it spreads

- Suspicious links or attachments.
- Malicious websites or untrusted applications.
- Infected USB devices.
- Compromised accounts or online sessions.

## Signs and protection

- Locked files, unfamiliar extensions, disabled security tools, or a ransom note are warning signs.
- Keep software updated, use security protection, enable multi-factor authentication, restrict permissions, and back up important data.
- If attacked, record the details, disconnect the device and other devices from the network, and change important passwords from a clean device. Do not assume payment guarantees recovery.
