Internet banking, UPI, cards, and mobile banking make payments convenient, but a security lapse can cause financial loss.

## UPI and payment safety

- Keep the UPI PIN secret and never share it.
- Remember that a UPI PIN is not required to receive money.
- Protect the device and payment application with a strong passcode.
- Verify the payee name and QR code before confirming a payment.

## Card safety

- Treat the card number, expiry date, CVV, and OTP as confidential.
- Never share card details or OTPs with anyone.
- Use cards only after checking the authenticity of the point-of-sale terminal, ATM, or website.
- Manage card limits through mobile banking for additional protection.

## Internet and mobile banking

- Use genuine and licensed operating systems for banking transactions.
- Verify banking URLs received by SMS or email before entering credentials.
- Use a strong banking password that is different from passwords for email, shopping, and other accounts.
- Avoid public computers and insecure internet connections for banking.
