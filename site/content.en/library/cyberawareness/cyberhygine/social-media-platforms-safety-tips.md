# Social Media Platforms - Safety Tips

<img src="/images/en/cyberawareness/cyberhygine/social-media-platforms-safety-tips.svg" width=700 alt="Social Media Platforms - Safety Tips" loading="lazy">

Use social media carefully to protect personal information, location details, and the authenticity of shared content.

## Do

- Choose privacy settings carefully before sharing content.
- Be cautious when revealing location information online.
- Verify friend requests before accepting them.
- Verify content before forwarding or sharing it.
- Enable multi-factor authentication on social media accounts.

## Do not

- Use a social media account without multi-factor authentication.
- Log in to social media accounts from untrusted systems.
- Share personal or location information without considering who can access it.
