Email messages can contain links that attempt to steal personal, financial, or organizational information.

## Safe practices

- Do not open or reply to links in emails that offer luring or unexpected benefits.
- Verify the sender before accessing a spam or suspicious email.
- Treat hyperlinks, web links, and URLs in email bodies with caution.
- Do not provide personal or financial details in response to an unverified email.
- Verify the authenticity of an email through a trusted channel before taking action.
