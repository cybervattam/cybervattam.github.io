__Financial Sector__
* Credit reports
* Peer-to-peer lending
* Peer-to-peer money transfers
* International transactions
* Reducing ledger-audit complexity
* Anti-money laundering and customer identity
* Trade finance and capital markets

__Healthcare Sector__
* Supply-chain authenticity for medicines, tablets, and medical equipment
* Electronic health records with transparent P2D and P2H transactions
* Evidence of diagnostic tests

__Insurance Sector__
* Improved multiparty contracts
* Streamlined risk-contract performance and claims settlement

__Legal Sector__
* Evidence authenticity
* Alternative case data
* Transparent chain of custody

__Media Sector__
* Ownership control
* Theft of digital assets
* Prevention of copyright infringement of digital assets
* Creator royalties
* Alternative ticket sales and reservations
    - Events
    - Movies
    - Workshops
    - Conferences
    - Seminars

__Transportation Sector__
* Passenger identity
* Boarding passes
* Passports
* Travel tickets
* Frequent traveler benefits management

__Real Estate__
* Will validation and inheritance allocation
* Transparent land-related agreements
* Landowner transfers and tracking

__Telecommunications Sector__
* Fraud prevention management
* Identity management
* Revenue assurance
* Mobile number portability
* Micropayments
* Roaming management

__Automotive Sector__
* Spare-parts manufacturing
* Vehicle history from manufacture through sale
* Supply-chain management
* Vehicle maintenance records
* Commercial vehicle tracking
* Alternative vehicle usage data

__Energy Sector__
* Peer-to-peer energy trading
* Smart meters
* Microgrids
* Virtual power plants

__E-Governance Sector__
* Authenticity of citizen registry data
* Citizen identity management
* Election voting
* Taxation

__Education Sector__
* Digitized education certificates, degrees, and diplomas
* Information repository for federated educational institutions

__Cross-Industry Use Cases__
* Background checks
* Identity verification
* Employment history
* Employee compensation and benefits
* Donations
* Charities
* Crowdfunding request tracking
* Stakeholder voting
